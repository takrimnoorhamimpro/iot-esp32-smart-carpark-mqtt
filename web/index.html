Smart Carpark Simulator

This page is the physical world. Cars drive on the road, stop at the gate sensors, type their booking code on the keypad and park in bays. Two ESP32 boards in Wokwi do the thinking: the gate controller reads the keypad and moves the barrier, the bay controller checks the code and guides the car.

Gate display

 
 

What the LCD at the gate shows right now. Keypad: 0-9 code, # confirm, * clear, A no booking. 3 wrong codes lock it for 30 s.

Drive

  1. Click a car waiting on the street. It drives to the IN sensor; manually press A on the gate keypad for walk-in entry.
  2. If the bay board says yes the barrier opens, the car drives through and follows the guidance to a free bay.
  3. Click a parked car to leave: it backs out, drives to the OUT sensor, waits for the gate and goes.
  4. The white car with the name tag is your car. Clicking it does nothing. It goes in only when you book a bay in the app and tap "Drive to the carpark". Then manually enter your 4-digit code on the keypad in the gate Wokwi diagram and press #. The gate board asks the bay board if the code is good; the simulator does not type the code for you.

Booking

How long a booked bay is held. The time starts when the person books in the app. If the car has not parked by then, the booking is released.
